Personal YouTube automation

These desktop scripts help the channel owner upload and manage videos on their own YouTube channels. They run on the owner's computer and use Google's OAuth consent flow to obtain permission. There is no public account registration or hosted user application.

Google OAuth app identities

  • Articles Admin — used by the Gracias por Hoy desktop YouTube scripts.
  • Doctor K YouTube Automation — used by the EmotionalResponses desktop YouTube scripts.
